# Why TriangleCCS exists

A genome is three readings of one object: a **string** on a finite alphabet, a
**leaf of a descent**, and a **point in a host**. Sequence data can keep the
first after losing the second (the balloon). A weighted tree code in hyperbolic
space need not pay an exponential tax to keep the second (Active Geometry
Theorem 4.4). This repository freezes a **polar chart** of that host so labs
can emit comparable addresses.

It is a **source–channel–decoder** artefact:

- **Source** — tree-structured retained genealogy.
- **Channel** — DNA/RNA as a tape that overwrites (site reuse).
- **Decoder** — this datum: packing bound, quartet classifier, polar H² chart.

Biology occupies the tape. Phylogenetics and Biosphere Atlas attempt the
decoder. Saturation (whether the source fills the chart) and curvature
genericity (whether H² is the unique symmetric medium) are detachable. Neither
is required for the datum to be useful.

A genomic language model is a **map of strings**, not the MDL of diversity.
The encoder that belongs here is the sextant (`docs/ENCODER.md`,
`triangleccs/sextant/place.py`): distances onto the frozen chart. Quartets
plus packing are the description length; the LM may register onto that chart
and must not author κ, n, or radius.

The four pieces of Active Geometry's throughline are the license:

1. Limit — packing.
2. Ladder — addresses ≠ genealogy.
3. Classifier — quartets.
4. Chart — inhabit H²; freeze κ as a gauge; train topology from quartets;
   radius from a named proxy; uniqueness up to O(2).
