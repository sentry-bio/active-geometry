# Paper II outline (v6) — *Evolution as Active Geometry*

Draft for review, not a manuscript. Paper I carries the machinery. This
paper cites it and instantiates it. v5 organized the biology as poverty
versus mixing. That cut is true and it is not the title claim. v6 is the
ladder paper: descent occupies the program's figure; genomes encode it;
sequence-\(\kappa\) was a fourth, illegal axis.

## The contribution

Descent is an addressing process in exponential room. A genome is a finite
encoding of that process, not the room it occupies. Independent
representations still look room-rich and tree-like except where independent
HGT instruments put them in the mixed cell. Sequence metrics lose the tree
in two ways the ladder already distinguished — erasure and reticulation —
which were previously averaged into a curvature. Absolute \(\kappa\) of life
is not a well-posed measurement of this geometry.

That is Layer I/IIa, instantiated. It is not Layer IIb.

## Title (proposed)

*Evolution as Active Geometry: Descent Occupies the Room; Genomes Encode It*

The first clause is the process — the geometry that is active. The second is
the ladder. The classifier and the autopsy of \(\kappa\) follow as
consequences, not as the name of the paper.

No state equation, no curvature constant, no atlas, no clock.

## The figure (one figure, three coordinates)

The honest empirical figure is already in
[`DECISIVE_EXPERIMENTS.md`](DECISIVE_EXPERIMENTS.md): growth class \(\times\)
tree defect, read from a representation that is not the inferred tree.

| | tree-like \(\delta\approx 0\) | mixed \(\delta>0\) |
|---|---|---|
| **exponential room** | vertical descent | HGT-rich descent |
| **polynomial room** | path-like | networked and cramped |

Paper I's limit says: if novelty is retained, life cannot live in the bottom
row. The classifier says: a given system sits in the left column or the
right. The ladder supplies the third coordinate, which is this paper:

**which object is being plotted — the generative process, or its encoding.**

Poverty is an apparent exit from the tree column, caused by reading the
encoding at depth. Mixing is a real one. Sequence-\(\kappa\) was a plot of
the encoding. It could not stabilize.

The geometry that is active is the process, used as lineages are made, not
fitted afterwards. A genome is how the addresses are written down.

## Inclusion rule

A result enters the spine only if it places biology on this figure, or names
how the encoding leaves it, on a declared object (process or encoding).
Simulations license the two encoding failures; they do not place a clade.
Absolute curvature, back-solved \(n\), and the state equation do not enter
the headline. Channel saturation (\(p\to 0.75\)) is never reported as
host-utilization saturation (\(\eta\to 1\)).

**Three holds, stated up front.**

1. **Occupancy, not force.** E9 (matched-capacity Euclidean vs hyperbolic)
   is unrun. §3 says the process sits in the top row. It does not say
   hyperbolic geometry is forced.
2. **Test B is unrun.** Misspecification without reticulation has not been
   run. Until it has, a non-recovering floor on real data cannot be called
   mixing rather than a wrong substitution model. The mixing *mechanism* is
   synthetic; the mixing *scar* is two independent HGT instruments.
3. **“Genealogy is expensive” is interpretation.** What is measured: the
   process occupies exponential room; the encoding loses the tree in two
   separable ways; mixing leaves a scar that packing need not share. The
   close reads those facts. It is labeled as a reading.

## Abstract — beats

1. Evolution is addressing-while-descending: a new lineage needs an identity
   that does not collide and a path back to its parent. Paper I is the
   solution class. This paper asks what life occupies, and what a genome is
   doing in that picture.
2. The honest figure is room \(\times\) tree. On ground truth, sequence
   encodings leave it in two ways. Poverty: quartets go quiet and recover
   with length — an apparent exit, the tape overwritten. Mixing: quartets
   go confidently wrong and do not recover; the transferred block is the
   donor's tree; packing need not fall — a real exit.
3. Independent of sequence distance at depth, the process still looks
   exponentially room-rich against a Euclidean null (KEGG; occupancy of the
   top row, not E9). Independent substitution entropy sits inside a
   nucleotide alphabet (\(1.76\)–\(2.17\) bits): the encoding is a tape, not
   a manifold.
4. Mixing is the mixed cell: HGT-enriched functions need extra angle; Atlas
   placement failures are \(7.7\times\) HGT-enriched. Same cell, two
   instruments.
5. Sequence-\(\kappa\) was a plot of the encoding. Tree-derived entropy
   correlates because it is the same object; chemistry does not; the stress
   objective cannot see scale. No curvature constant is claimed.

## §2 The figure on ground truth

Not a biological placement. The license for how encodings leave the figure.

- **§2.1 The two exits.** Reading a sequence metric as a tree can fail by
  erasure (apparent exit from the tree column) or by reticulation (real
  exit). Packing class is a different axis: \(\delta\perp h_{\mathrm{pack}}\)
  is certified on synthetics (M4 tracks reticulation at \(r=0.946\),
  packing does not, \(r=0.039\)).
- **§2.2 Poverty (Test A).** JC69, exact Yule genealogy. Block witness
  count stays near the ceiling. Decided-quartet accuracy rises with \(L\)
  at \(D=2\) and \(D=3\) through \(400{,}000\) sites, no flattening.
  Undecidable fraction shrinks on a \(\ln L\) schedule. Infinite-sites at
  matched event count does not collapse. Mechanism: site reuse, not “more
  mutations.” Molecular-channel saturation, not host-utilization
  saturation. The tree is still in the generating process; the tape is too
  short.
- **§2.3 Mixing (Test C).** Non-saturating depth, HGT rate swept. Block
  witness count drifts *up* (\(247\to 254\)). Undecidable fraction is
  identically zero. Decided accuracy floors under \(16\times L\).
  Transferred block vs donor topology \(98.7\%\); vs recipient \(52.3\%\).
  It does not get ambiguous. It gets confidently misdirected.
- **§2.4 The diagnostic.** \(L\)-recovery: if decided accuracy climbs, the
  encoding is poor; if it sits, the encoding is mixed — *provided* Test B
  has ruled out misspecification. Status: reported local simulations;
  reproduce in-repo before citing tables in a submitted draft.

## §3 Occupancy of the top row

## §5 The illegal plot

Not a curvature section. Why sequence-\(\kappa\) was not a coordinate on
the figure.

- **§5.1 The type error.** \(\kappa\) was asked to read the host off the
  encoding. Quiet encodings and mixed encodings demand opposite responses
  (more tape versus a different object). Averaging them cannot converge.
- **§5.2 Shared input, isolated.** Same 15 protein families, four entropy
  estimators vs \(\kappa\): \(H_{\mathrm{branch}}\) \(r=+0.74\),
  \(h_{\mathrm{aln}}\) \(+0.69\), \(h_{\mathrm{tree}}\) \(+0.63\),
  \(h_{\mathrm{LG}}\) (never touches the tree) \(+0.29\) (\(p=0.29\)).
  Correlation is monotone in shared input. Tree-derived \(h\) correlates
  because it is the same object. This is the headline mechanism of the
  retired viral \(r=0.996\), which is **not cited as a result** (6-point
  Wolfram set including CMV and “All Life”; authors' own E-beta kill
  triggered on 3 of 4 independent estimators).
- **§5.3 Stress does not identify \(\kappa\).** Protein v1 vs v2, identical
  distance matrices, essentially identical stress, \(\kappa\) shifts
  \(2.3\)–\(4.2\times\) on the nine well-behaved families. Same defect as
  `scale_pinned: false` (log-log slope \(-0.11\) vs \(-1\)). The objective
  cannot see scale, so it cannot see curvature. Against v1 curvatures none
  of the four entropy estimators correlate. v1 and v2 are different
  measurements, not a bugfix of one.
- **§5.4 The IIb bet, left open.** Independent domain \(h\) from §3.3 vs
  published \(\kappa\): \(r=0.355\), below the pre-registered kill of
  \(0.5\). On synthetics, the stress estimator behind every published
  \(\kappa\) misses known \(\kappa_{\mathrm{true}}=(\ln b)^2\) by

- **§5.5 Comparative leftovers, if kept at all.** Viral relative curvature

  \(\mathbb H^2\): exponential. Grids and Euclidean MSTs: polynomial.
  \(13/13\), no overlap. Makes Corollary 4.3 enforceable. M3 magnitude is
  **not** certified; \(\eta\) within \(\pm 20\%\) of 1 is uninterpretable
  and is not reported.
- **R5 ball-count recovery and Euclidean null.** Reproduces at \(1.08\%\)
  and \(\hat\kappa=0\). Load-bearing correction: this certifies
  *ball-counting*, which produced none of the published \(\kappa\). One
  sentence.
- **R1 Procrustes.** Real checkpoints, \(r=0.948\pm 0.016\), \(\kappa\)
  fixed. Destroyed-structure control. Stability inside the imposed model,
  not host-class selection.
- **Gauge.** Scale-blindness of relative-stress \(\kappa\) (log-log slope
  \(-0.11\); deliberate rescaling invisible). Independent-\(h\) required
  for any capacity number in the manuscript.
- **Minimal encoder.** Imposed \(\mathbb H^2\), residual \(0.020\), gauge
  \(O(2)\). Polar \(\mathbb H^2\) is the embeddability candidate of the
  *process*, frozen because contrastive temperature and curvature are

- **InfoNCE degeneracy.** One clause, no reconstructed-loss table.

## §7 Discussion — the throughline

**The reading, labeled as interpretation.** Life keeps two things in one
place: what a thing is (a distinguishable address) and where it came from
(a path). Those are not stored in the same geometry. Identity is an address
on the tape; genealogy is a path in the room. Paper I's ladder says these
are different capacities. The evidence says they degrade independently.
Block capacity is robust: genomes stay distinguishable through channel
saturation and through transfer; Test C's witness count even rises, because
mixing makes sequences more distinct from their vertical neighbors, not
less. Relational capacity is fragile in two separable ways. A phylogeny is
not a sequence metric. It is a reconstruction of a physical quantity with a
measurable ceiling, hit either by erosion (fixable) or by structural
contradiction (not). Reticulation is a second tree competing for the same
tape, not noise in a first tree.

**Claimed (I/IIa).** Occupancy of exponential room on an independent
functional representation (candidate, not E9); two encoding failures on
ground truth; two HGT scars in real data, consistent with the mixed cell
rather than with poverty; independent \(h\) inside the alphabet; sequence-
\(\kappa\) not a well-posed coordinate.

**Not claimed.** That E9 is done; that a real-data floor is mixing rather
than misspecification; that “genealogy is expensive” is a measurement; A3;
saturation; a filled atlas; CCS as host-class proof; any \(\kappa\)
constant; that hyperbolic geometry is forced.

**Falsifiability.** (i) E9 matched-capacity fails on independent real data
\(\to\) host class fails (occupancy was not force); (ii) HGT instruments
disagree in sign once recA is re-derived without back-solving \(\to\) mixed
cell fails; (iii) \(L\)-recovery on a known-HGT alignment climbs to 1
\(\to\) the floor prediction fails for that system; (iv) Test B floors
without HGT \(\to\) the diagnostic does not yet distinguish mixing from a
wrong model; (v) independent functional geometry looks polynomial against a
Euclidean null \(\to\) the top-row occupancy fails.

**Close, not with “biology is active geometry.”** That is the IIb claim
this paper brackets. Close with:

> Life keeps identity cheaply and genealogy expensively because they are
> not stored in the same geometry. Identity is an address on the tape;
> genealogy is a path in the room. When the encoding fails the process, it
> fails quietly, which more data repairs, or wrongly, which it does not.
> The geometry that is active is the room. The genome is the writing.

That is Layer I/IIa, it is what the evidence supports, and it leaves the
state equation unearned.
